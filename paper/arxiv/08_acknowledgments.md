# Acknowledgments
This work stands on infrastructure the prime-gap community
built over decades. The record tables were founded and curated
until his death in 2019 by Thomas R. Nicely [nicely1999], and
are continued today by the Prime Gap List Project
[primegaplist]; we thank Dana Jacobsen for his long stewardship
of that list and its tooling. Seth Troisi's combined sieve
[troisi2020] outperforms our own and remains the field's
reference, and his verification portal serves as the
community's independent notary for every submitted record. The
published artifacts of H\"oglund, Raab, Jacobsen and Anderson,
Smith, and Renyer form the retrodiction testbed of Section 5;
naming them records our debt to their years of computation and
implies no endorsement by them. The maximal-merit record priced
in Section 6 was set by the Gapcoin proof-of-work network
[gapcoin2017]. Our verification chain relies on Robert
Gerbicz's error check, the OpenPFGW project, and the GNU MP
library. Research assistance throughout was provided by Claude
(Anthropic); its preregistered hypotheses -- including the five
refuted ones -- and its corrected errors appear by name and
date in Appendix A and in the public ledger, which we regard as
the substantive form of the required AI-assistance disclosure.
This work received no external funding; computations ran on the
author's own and rented consumer hardware.
