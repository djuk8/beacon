# 4. Verification

## 4.1 Method
Every quantitative claim in this paper passed through one
pipeline: PREREGISTER (dated hypothesis, named author -- the AI
assistant's predictions are so named -- with numeric pass bands)
-> MEASURE (on gated instruments) -> FILE (PASS or REFUTED,
verbatim, undeleted). Instruments are admitted only after (i) a
known-answer corpus, (ii) bit-exact equivalence against an
independent implementation, and (iii) a fault-injection drill; a
green gate on code the gate does not cover is treated as worse
than no gate. The pricing core carries a three-anchor fingerprint
executed at every import; drift aborts the caller.

## 4.2 Independence
Survivor counts are produced by three unrelated routes: the
production landing-arithmetic sieve; a dense boolean sieve
written as a deliberately different code path and byte-compared
per candidate; and the analytic convolution (2.4). Agreement of
the third with the first two, across the domain of 3.6, is the
central empirical fact of this paper. Public submissions add a
fourth, adversarial route: the community notary re-proves both
endpoints and re-sieves the interior on independent hardware.

## 4.3 The verdict chain
Fifteen preregistered hypotheses were filed in the discovery arc;
FIVE WERE REFUTED, all authored by the AI assistant, each
refutation locating the next structure: the off-shelf failure of
the exponential form exposed the second coordinate; the failure
of the saturation ansatz exposed the filling; the failure of the
naive lip proof exposed the channel staircase; the failure of
every scalar coordinate established the surface. The full table
-- statements, bands, dates, verdicts -- is Appendix A. We regard
the refuted rows as results.

## 4.4 Precision attained
Parity-exact bridge vs exhaustive sieves: sub-1% for zd >= 331,
<= 3% at zd = 79. Campaign anchors: monument +0.1%, strike -0.0%,
record +0.2%. Production ring ladder: all seven rings within
0.8%, including the smooth-zone ring 1; the theater's ln n0
reproduced to one part in 10^4. Published records: Section 5.
Q-invariance (2.5i): 0.24% measured drift across a decade of
sieve depth.

## 4.5 The cost statistic, and a survivorship warning
Windows-to-success is geometric with mean e^E -- a consequence of
the law, not an added model -- so all cost figures are exact
quantiles: median = ln 2 * e^E; 80%-budget = 2.32x median; lucky
quartile = 0.415x median. A prior operating band, "measured at
the frontier," proved on audit to span the 12th-39th percentiles:
banked campaigns are the lucky ones, and bands measured on
survivors inherit their luck. The band was retired to the ledger;
we report medians and quantiles only.

## 4.6 Not certified, and said so
The following ship as flagged models, not measurements: the
NVIDIA 4090 rate ladder (x0.60 of the gated 5090 ladder, pending
a one-hour gate run any reader can perform); theta(zd) beyond
10^6 (PNT approximation); desert ratios z outside [0.98, 1.03];
CPU seconds-per-test beyond its three anchors; the ladder-factor
bookkeeping against deployed fold statistics (one reconciliation
pending). A number without a flag in this paper is exact or
measured; a number with a flag tells you which kind of caution it
carries.
